### Where the deferrals concentrate, and why that is honest

Sixteen of the 22 deferrals belong to **another phase's surface** — inventory (`P1-21`), quotations
(`P1-20`), reception (`P1-18`/`P1-28`), the platform (realtime, subscription gating), or the P1-29
**Frontend** slice. P1-29 consumes those contracts; it does not own them, and a backend remediation
plan that silently absorbed them would be widening a phase that is already mixed.

The remaining six are Owner decisions or genuinely unscoped modelling — `INS-29`, `INS-30`,
`INS-31`, `INS-38`, `INS-48`, `INS-15` — and each is visible in
[screen-to-contract-matrix.md](screen-to-contract-matrix.md) as a **REFUSED** or **OWNER** action, so
no screen can quietly imply the capability exists.

**`INS-38` is the one a reviewer should look at hardest.** It is structurally the same finding as
the phase's largest Critical, and it is deferred only because no Owner requirement depends on it.
If that judgement is wrong, the answer is `BR-10`, shaped exactly like `BR-04`.
