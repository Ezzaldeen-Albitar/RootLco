## Concurrency race summary

Single-winner races proven across isolated repetitions on non-privileged login roles:
last-unit reservation (loser `23514`), duplicate issued revision (partial-unique),
`record_item_decision` under a superseding revision, opening/adjustment approval, and
idempotent reservation replay (returns the existing row, never `23505`). Accepted
loser SQLSTATEs: `23505`, `23514`, `23P01`.
