Regression round two found six half-finished repairs of this decision: one line
of a document corrected and another line of the SAME document left saying the
opposite. This ledger exists so the next reader does not have to re-derive which
is which, and so a grep result is never mistaken for a verdict.

opposite defect. That is why this is a classification and not a sweep.

## What was NOT touched, and why

- **Superseded phase evidence.** `docs/phase-1/phase-1-27/evidence/**` and the
  closed phases before it record what was true when they were sealed. Rewriting
  them to match the present would destroy the only record of the assumption the
  product was built on, and every digest over them besides.
- **The Owner-facing product corpus, where it is right.** Most of it is, for the
  reason above: the decision it names is still open for the surface it names.
- **The identity of the decision.** `frontend-implementation-program.md` §928
  records that issuing or amending an open-decision identifier is the Owner’s
  act and that `docs/` holds dispositions only. Nothing here issues, amends or
  closes `P1-OD-025`; it reports the scope of an answer already given and
  already built, and points at the seven rows that constitute it.
